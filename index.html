Frequently asked questions

Are these real listings?

No. The built-in catalogue is sample data for demonstration. Listings you create are stored only in your browser.

Does checkout place a real order?

No. Checkout creates a local demo order ID. It does not contact a seller or arrange delivery.

Does PostBooks verify UPI payments?

No. This static page can open a UPI app and generate a QR payment link, but it cannot securely confirm payment. Always verify the payment in your own UPI app before delivering anything.

Can my listing be seen on another phone?

No. Cross-device listings, shared carts, real customer accounts, and order management require a hosted backend and database.

Privacy notice

PostBooks is a front-end demo. Your created listings, cart, wishlist, and demonstration orders are saved with localStorage in this browser on this device. They are not uploaded to a server. Anyone with access to this browser profile may be able to view this stored demo data.

Do not enter passwords, banking credentials, identity numbers, or sensitive personal information. The sell form's contact field is saved locally as part of your listing; leaving it blank is recommended for public demos.

External book cover photos and the QR-code service may receive ordinary connection metadata from their respective providers when loaded.

UPI payment information

This demo can create a UPI payment URI for the displayed order total and ask your device to open a compatible payment app. Availability depends on the device and installed apps.

Important: payment is not verified by this website. The page cannot receive a reliable payment result, prevent fraudulent claims, send seller notifications, or reconcile payments. For real commerce, integrate a suitable payment provider or a secure payment-verification backend.

The recipient UPI ID configured for this demo is 6393871933@fam. Check recipient and amount in your UPI app before authorizing any transaction.
